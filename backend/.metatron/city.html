Omega City

A place rather than a picture. Drag to orbit, scroll to fly in, click any tower to travel to it. Each tower is a module, each floor a real directory, and the vertical axis is the request path — you arrive at the roof and descend to the database.

02

Reading the city

The amber pylons are deviations from the rules the repo writes down for itself, sited on the module they belong to. The arcs overhead are cross-domain dependencies.

03

Taking it to Claude

Selecting a tower fills the Context block in the panel with a plain-text digest of exactly what you are looking at — its floors, its absent tiers, its deviations and its dependencies. Copy it into a conversation and the model has the same view you do, without you narrating the codebase from memory.

04

What is real, and what is staging

The renderer is hand-written: a perspective camera, back-face culling and painter's-algorithm depth sorting on canvas. There is no depth buffer, so if two towers ever interpenetrate the sort could pick wrong — they are laid out so they never do.
